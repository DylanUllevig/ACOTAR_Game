== ch08_fountain

N He had taken my weapons. There had to be an armory somewhere, with all those knives on his chest. And if not, I would steal something. Just in case.

== ch08_pool
N A long reflecting pool under blossom trees, lily pads drifting on it. Open lawn all around. Nowhere to hide here, and anyone at the windows could see me.
N Last night I'd found there was no lock on my window, and wisteria thick as rope climbing the wall beneath it. Not that I meant to run. But it was good to know how I could.

== ch08_stables
N Stables, painted and pretty, the stable hands in horse masks. Not one of them looked at me. Maybe they blamed me for Andras. I didn't blame them.
N Horses meant a way to cover ground. If I ever had to.

== ch08_gate

N South, beyond it, lay the wall. And my family. I made myself turn around.
