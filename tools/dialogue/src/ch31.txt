== ch31_late

@father/neutral I've half a mind to buy the Beddor estate. It will go up for sale before long, now there's no one to inherit it. Good land, too. One of you might want a house of your own there one day.

@elain/sad It happened just the day before you came back to us.
@feyre/afraid Clare Beddor.
@elain/neutral Clare. She was a friend of ours, years ago, before we lost everything. You remember.
N I nodded. I could feel Nesta watching me.
N It couldn't be. It had to be chance. Because the alternative... In Tamlin's dining room, with Rhysand's mind pressing at mine, I had needed a name. Any name but my own. And I had given him hers.
N He had not forgotten it.

@feyre/determined If you won't go, then pay for guards. Pay men to keep watch on the Wall, and the woods, and the village.

@feyre/determined And the moment anything seems wrong, any rumour that the Wall has been crossed, anything odd at all, you take a ship and leave. Sail south, as far as the ships go. Somewhere no faerie would bother with.
