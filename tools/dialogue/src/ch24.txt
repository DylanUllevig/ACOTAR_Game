N A shadow moved behind his eyes.

@tamlin/weary Yes. They'd never known freedom, or the seasons as you know them. They didn't know what to do with themselves out in the mortal world. But most of them were very, very happy to go. I was happy to see them go. Even if my father wasn't.

@feyre/tender You're not your father, Tamlin. Or your brothers. You never once made me feel like a prisoner. Or like a piece of property.
