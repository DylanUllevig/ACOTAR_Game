N The birdsong became an orchestra. Gossip and mirth, theme and variation, more layers of music than I had ever heard in my life. And beneath it all, a woman's voice, weary and sad. The willow.
N I opened my eyes. Everything was richer, clearer. The brook ran like a rainbow over stones smooth as silk. The trees wore a faint shimmer that danced out to the edge of every leaf. Magic didn't stink of metal anymore. It smelled of jasmine, and lilac, and roses.
N I would never be able to paint it. Pieces of it, maybe. Never the whole. It broke my heart.
N Then I looked at Tamlin, and my heart broke the rest of the way.
N His skin had a golden sheen, and a circlet of sunlight glowed about his head. His eyes weren't simply green and gold, but every shade a forest could hold. This was a High Lord of Prythian. Devastating. Powerful beyond belief.
