== ch19_paint
N Weeks went by, and the days melted together. Most of what I painted was awful. I never let anyone see it, however much Tamlin prodded or Lucien smirked at the paint on my clothes.
N Some days I rode out with Tamlin to see his lands and came home with ideas that had me out of bed at dawn. Other days he was called to the borders and came back in blood that wasn't his. He never told me about it, and I never asked. That he came back was enough.
N I still dreamed of the deaths I'd watched, and the ones I'd dealt, and of a pale woman pulling me to pieces while a shadow I could never make out looked on. But bit by bit, the fear let go of me. Keep to his side, the Suriel had said. So I did.
N And then one morning, past the outer hedge, a warm wind blew up from the south. Spring was coming to the mortal lands.
N My family, glamoured and fed and safe, didn't know where I was. Life had gone on without me. To them, I might as well have been in my grave. And he had let me stop thinking about them.
N I didn't paint that day. I sat in front of a blank canvas with no colors in me at all. Maybe the paints had only ever been a way to keep me quiet. To stop me asking. And I had stopped, like a good, obedient human.
N I made it through a tense dinner, and when it was over I went out into the dusk and lost myself among the hedges.
