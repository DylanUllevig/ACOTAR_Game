== ch13_mural
> set_flag ch13_mural
N Not a painting. A mural. The word came back to me from somewhere half forgotten.
N It began with a cauldron: a great black cauldron held by slender, glowing hands in an endless starry night, tipped so that something golden spilled from it into the dark and pooled into our world.
N Then a map of everything, the seas and the far continents, all of it theirs. No sign of humans anywhere. We'd been as low as pigs to them.
N Then a battlefield. Rows of mortal soldiers in bloody mud, facing the faerie hordes about to break over them. The next panel was only a smear of black, flecked with red. It said enough.
N Then a smaller faerie realm, and a dark, ugly smear across the bottom of our island. The wall. South of it, the painter hadn't bothered with anything at all.
N Just above the wall, the court that had given up the most land to us: painted with crocuses and lambs and roses. Spring.
N Above it, six more. Autumn, Summer, Winter. Dawn in soft reds, Day in gold and blue. And at the top, sprawling over frozen mountains, darkness and stars. The Night Court. Things lived in the shadows between those peaks. Little eyes. Gleaming teeth.
N At the heart of it all rose one bare, lonely mountain, without snow, without life, as if the very weather wouldn't touch it.
@feyre/afraid Never go north. Whatever happens, never go north.
