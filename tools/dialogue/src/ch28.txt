N I woke angry, to hooves ringing on flagstones. Why put me to sleep at all? So I wouldn't see the way back?
N Outside the window, a long drive sloped up between clipped hedges shaped like green flames, with irises crowded along its edges. At its head stood a house of white marble under emerald roofs, nearly as large as the manor I'd left. I had never seen it in my life.
N The footman who handed me down had round ears and a ruddy, sunburned face. Human. Every servant on those steps was human: fidgeting, blinking, breathing loudly, so restless after the stillness of the High Fae. They shrank from me as if I were someone grand.
