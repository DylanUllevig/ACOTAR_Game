N I woke panting. The Suriel's clicking fingers. Grinning naga. A pale, faceless woman dragging blood-red nails across my throat, asking my name, and every time I tried to answer, my own blood choked me.
N And then a different sound, creeping under my door from the front hall. Shouts, giving orders. And screaming.
N I'd heard screams like that in the woods at home, when I didn't make a clean kill. I couldn't bear it. And I had to know.

== ch17_hand
N I took his hand. His long fingers wrapped round mine and covered them completely. I brushed the damp hair off his face. Black, pleading eyes found mine.
@faerie/neutral My wings.
@feyre/sad It will be all right. You'll get them back.
@faerie/neutral You swear?
@feyre/sad Yes.
N He smiled a little, and closed his eyes. The first false vow I had ever sworn.
N Tamlin took his other hand and began to pray, in words older than the mortal lands.
@tamlin/weary Cauldron keep you. Mother hold you. Go through the gates, into the deathless land of milk and honey. Fear nothing there. Feel no pain. Go on, into forever.
N The faerie sighed once, and his hand went slack in mine.
N I didn't let go. I didn't know how long it took for a soul to leave a body. I stood in his blood until it went cold, still stroking his hair, wondering if he'd known I lied. Wondering whether, wherever he'd gone, he had his wings again.
@tamlin/tender He's gone. Let him go.
N I tucked his hair behind his long ear, wishing I'd known his name, and let go.
N Tamlin led me to the foot of the stairs. Bloody footprints followed us.
@feyre/sad We can't leave him there.
@tamlin/weary I know. I'll see to him. Alone.
N No claws, no fangs. There was nothing here for him to fight.
@tamlin/tender Feyre. Why? You don't like my kind on a good day. And after Andras... Why?
@feyre/sad Because I wouldn't want to die alone. I'd want someone holding my hand until the end, and a while after. Everyone deserves that, human or faerie.
@feyre/sad I regret what I did to Andras. I regret that there was so much hate in me. I wish I could undo it. I'm sorry. I'm so sorry.
N I couldn't remember the last time I had said anything like that to anyone. He only nodded, and turned away.
N From the window at the top of the stairs, I watched him carry the broken body out through the moonlit garden and into the fields beyond. He never once looked back.
